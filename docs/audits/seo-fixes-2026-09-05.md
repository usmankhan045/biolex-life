## What changed

### Critical

**19 orphaned posts recovered.** `/blog` and `/category/[slug]` both exported a
static `metadata` object. A static export cannot vary by `searchParams`, so every
`?page=N` inherited the page-1 canonical and Google discounted the whole
pagination chain. Category archives compounded it with a hard `limit: 50` and no
pagination UI, stranding 10 home-organization and 9 wall-art posts.

Both routes now use `generateMetadata` with per-page canonicals; category
archives paginate at 24/page with real prev/next controls and a true total count.

Verified: 60 of 60 home-organization and 58 of 58 wall-art posts reachable
(wall-art is 58, not 59, because of the merge below).

**Legal pages.** `privacy-policy`, `terms-of-use`, and `disclaimer` were
`noindex` while sitemap-submitted. Removed the `noindex` rather than the sitemap
entries: these are the pages quality raters check, and the sibling legal pages
were already indexable.

**Search results** (`/blog?q=`) are now `noindex, follow` instead of indexable
near-duplicates of `/blog`.

### High

- **Related printables**: took the first 3 of an unsorted global list, so all 90
  detail pages recommended the same 3 files. Now category-filtered with a
  slug-rotated backfill. Same fix for the companion-article fallback, which had
  most printables pointing at one article.
- **Author archive**: capped at 100 of 208 while claiming to list everything.
- **Blog card titles**: `sr-only` whenever a post had a featured image, so cards
  rendered with no visible headline.
- **Answer blocks**: all 49 sections over 240 words rewritten (worst was 354).
  Each stated its answer, then repeated it in a closing summary; the repetition
  went and the substance stayed. Every `{{printable:}}` shortcode and internal
  link was preserved and verified programmatically.
- **Outbound citations**: 66 posts gained one verified citation on a claim they
  already made. The remaining 142 make no checkable claim and were left alone.

### Medium / Low

Cannibalizing wall-art pair merged (301 + inbound links repointed + weaker post
unpublished); category intro copy added; category titles retargeted from
`"X: Blog"`; `llms.txt` regenerated from the real corpus with `llms-full.txt`
added; sitemap `lastmod` and truncation guard; dead CTA section guarded;
`Product`/`Offer` and `ItemList` schema; `ImageObject` dimensions; long
`seo_title` shortened; `/about` H1 and title made descriptive.

## Two audit findings that were wrong

Worth recording so they don't get re-litigated.

**IndexNow was already implemented.** The audit flagged it as missing. It is
fully shipped: `scripts/indexnow-submit.mjs` posts to `api.indexnow.org`, and the
key file is live at `/8e0f08cab3c95c0f7422368a8ce4b6a6.txt` (HTTP 200). The
original check looked for a file named "indexnow" and missed it. No work needed.

**The "closing H2 is the worst offender" pattern was a small-sample artifact.**
Two agents independently reported the final section as the consistent overshoot.
Measured across all 1,248 sections, final sections average 187 words and other
sections average 200, so the overshoot was actually uniform. The extreme tail
*was* concentrated at the end (40 of the 49 worst were final sections), which is
what the sampling picked up. The fix targeted the tail, which was correct either
way.

## Still open

**The strategic question (C3) is deliberately untouched.** The audit found 6 of 6
head queries are won by gallery/download-first pages while Biolex ships
1,350-word articles, and that `/category/printable-wall-art` links 50 articles
and zero printables. Every fix above helps regardless of which way that goes, but
inverting the architecture is a business decision about what the site *is*, not a
bug fix. The cheap test still stands: rebuild one category page as a thumbnail
grid linking to `/free-printables/`, and measure it against the other four.

**Apex redirect chain (M1).** `http://biolexlife.com` still takes two hops to
reach `https://www.biolexlife.com`. This is Vercel domain configuration, not
application code, so it needs to be changed in the project's domain settings.

**No Google API data.** Core Web Vitals, Search Console indexation, and GA4
traffic remain unmeasured. Setup steps are in the audit doc. Until this exists,
any performance claim is unverified.

**117 sections are under 134 words.** Now the larger deviation from the target
band. These were short before this work and were not touched, since padding a
tight answer to hit a word count is the opposite of the goal.
