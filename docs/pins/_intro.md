# Paper Posy, Pinterest Pin Bank

Every published post gets **3 pins**, each a different hook/angle but all linking
to the **same destination: the blog article** (never the raw PDF, never the
`/free-printables` page). This is deliberate, Pinterest traffic must land on the
full, ad-supported guide first; the printable download sits 2+ clicks deeper
inside the article. Outbound clicks to the article are the only KPI that pays.

> **This file is the single source of truth for pins.** The content fan-out
> appends a `### <post-slug>` block per post (3 pins each). A generator turns
> these blocks + the cadence rules below into a day-by-day posting calendar.

---

## Posting cadence, 6 pins/day, from 6 different posts

The rule: **post 6 pins a day, each from a different post, and space a single
post's 3 pins 2 days apart** so every post gets three separate reach windows
instead of one.

Because each post yields 3 pins and we post 6/day, the funnel consumes **2 new
posts per day**. In steady state each day's 6 pins are:

| Slot | Pin | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | **Pin A** | the 2 posts *introduced today* |
| 3-4 | **Pin B** | the 2 posts introduced *2 days ago* |
| 5-6 | **Pin C** | the 2 posts introduced *4 days ago* |

→ 6 pins, 6 distinct posts, and any one post's A/B/C land on day *d*, *d+2*, *d+4*.

**Rules that don't change (from the master plan):**
- **Size:** 1000 × 1500 px (2:3). Taller pins get cropped and demoted.
- **Never dump** all 3 of a post's pins on one day. 3-5 pins/day is the safe
 floor; 6/day is our target while the bank is fresh.
- **Enable Rich Pins** (~13% higher CTR).
- **Manually add your own tags** to every pin so Pinterest's auto product tags
 don't hijack the image click away from your site.
- **Keyword first** in both title and description, the keyword is the most
 important element of the pin. Use the post's target keyword verbatim.
- Start pinning a post **only after its article is live** (published by the
 drip-publisher). Pinning lags publishing, which is healthy for Pinterest.
- **Refresh:** every article gets NEW pins in months 3, 6, 9. An article is never "done".

## Pin image prompt style guide

Every pin's ChatGPT/image prompt should produce a **1000×1500 vertical Pinterest
graphic** that is:
- **Light-themed** (warm cream `#FCFBF7` background), matches the site, reads clean in-feed.
- **On-brand accents:** grape violet `#5B34E0`, electric lime `#C9F24E`, deep sage `#7A9E7E`.
- **Design, not photo:** show a *mockup of the actual printable* (a letter-size
 sheet with the schedule/checklist visible) on a soft flat-lay or framed board,
 plus a bold text overlay. This shows the payload and reads as a made asset.
- **Big legible headline** carrying the keyword, a smaller supporting line, and a
 small "paperposy.com" wordmark + a lime "Free Printable" pill.
- Fonts: a bold grotesque headline + clean sans supporting text.
- No stock-photo clutter, no people's faces, no brand logos.
