## What sizes and layouts work best for a celestial gallery wall?

Match the print size to the wall and the number of pieces, and the arrangement almost designs itself. A single statement moon looks best at 11x14 or larger above a bed or sofa. A tidy pair suits a narrow space like a hallway or between windows. A three-piece set is the easiest balanced gallery wall for most rooms. Here is a quick guide to what fits where:

| Layout | Best size per print | Works well in |
|---|---|---|
| Single statement piece | 11x14 or 16x20 | Above bed, sofa, console |
| Matching pair | 8x10 each | Hallway, between windows, nightstands |
| Three-piece set | 8x10 or 5x7 | Living room, nursery, gallery wall |
| Mini cluster (4 to 6) | 5x7 | Reading nook, stairwell, desk |

Keep 2 to 3 inches of space between frames so the group reads as one composition. For a set, hang the middle piece at eye level (about 57 to 60 inches to center) and build outward. When you are working above furniture, let the artwork span roughly two-thirds the width of the sofa or headboard below it, so the grouping feels anchored rather than floating. Consistent framing does more for a polished look than any single print.
