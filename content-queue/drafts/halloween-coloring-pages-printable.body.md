## What is the best way to print Halloween coloring pages at home?

Print on standard letter-size paper at 100% scale, choose "actual size," not "fit to page," so the design keeps its clean proportions and the outlines stay crisp. Set your printer to grayscale or black-and-white to save color ink, since a coloring page only needs sharp black lines to work. If the page comes out faint, bump the print quality up one notch from draft to normal; the darker the outlines, the easier they are for little hands to follow.

For pages your kids will color with markers, reach for slightly heavier paper, 24 lb instead of the usual 20 lb copy paper, so the ink does not bleed or wrinkle. One quick tip that saves a lot of grief: print a small stack at once while the printer is already warmed up, then tuck the spares in a folder. That way the next "I'm bored" moment is solved before it starts, and you are not scrambling to reload the printer during a party or a meltdown.

## What supplies make Halloween coloring more fun?

The paper matters far less than what your kids color with.
