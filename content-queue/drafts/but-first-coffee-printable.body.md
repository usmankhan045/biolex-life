## What sizes does it come in, and how do you print it at home?

Most printables bundle several standard sizes in one download, usually 5x7, 8x10, and 11x14, so you can match the print to the wall instead of forcing the wall to fit the print. To get a clean result at home, print your but first coffee printable at the highest quality setting on medium-weight matte cardstock (around 60-80 lb, or 160-216 gsm), choose "actual size" or "100 percent" in the print dialog rather than "fit to page" (which quietly shrinks the image and adds uneven borders), and run one test on plain paper first to check the framing. Standard printer paper works in a pinch, but cardstock is what separates a homemade page from a gallery print, since it holds color better and will not ripple inside a frame.
