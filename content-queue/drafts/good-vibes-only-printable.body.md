## How do you print a good vibes only sign so it looks store-bought?

Print on heavier paper and keep the design at its true size, and a homemade sign stops looking homemade. Plain copy paper reads thin and curls inside a frame, so reach for a pack of matte photo paper or 65-to-100 lb cardstock, which gives the print a substantial, gallery feel for just a few cents a sheet. In your print dialog, set scaling to "Actual size" or 100 percent instead of "Fit," so the proportions stay true and the margins land where the design intends them to. Choose "High" or "Best" quality, and if your printer offers borderless printing, use it for a cleaner edge. Let the sheet dry for a minute before framing so nothing smudges. That is the entire trick. Nice paper plus true-to-size settings is the whole difference between "printed at home" and "where did you buy that?" Everything else is just choosing a frame.
