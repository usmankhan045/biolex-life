## How do you arrange a gallery wall above your desk?

Lay the whole arrangement on the floor first, then hang from the center outward, keeping 2-3 inches of even spacing between frames. A desk gallery wall works best when the pieces sit as a tight cluster rather than drifting across a large blank wall, so the collection reads as one anchored unit above your workspace. Start by centering your favorite or largest print at roughly eye level when seated, then build the others around it in a loose grid or a simple row. Keep the gaps consistent, uneven spacing is the single thing that makes a gallery wall look accidental. Cut paper templates the size of each frame and tape them to the wall first, so you can adjust before putting a single hole in the drywall. Aim for the center of the arrangement to land at seated eye level, roughly 57-60 inches from the floor, since a wall hung too high floats away from the desk and breaks the connection.
