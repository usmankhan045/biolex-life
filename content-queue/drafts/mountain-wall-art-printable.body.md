## What paper and print settings make mountain line art look professional?

The single biggest upgrade is heavier paper. Standard 20-lb copy paper works in a pinch, but a matte presentation stock or cardstock in the 32-lb to 65-lb range gives your print a gallery weight that does not buckle or ripple inside a frame. For crisp black mountain line art, set your printer to its highest quality setting, choose the "matte" or "presentation" paper type, and print in grayscale so the blacks stay true instead of turning slightly green or purple. Here is a quick settings checklist to run before you print:

- Scale: 100 percent or "actual size" (never "fit to page")
- Quality: Best or High
- Paper type: Matte, Presentation, or Cardstock
- Color: Grayscale for black-and-white designs
- Margins: Borderless if your printer allows, or trim the white edge after

If your home printer maxes out at thin paper, the self-serve machines at an office-supply store will run a single sheet on heavy stock for well under a dollar, and the difference in how the finished piece feels is immediate.

## How do you frame printable mountain wall art so it looks expensive?

Framing is where a free download starts to look like a fifty-dollar print. The trick is a simple frame plus a wide mat. A thin oak, black, or white frame with a generous white mat board instantly reads as gallery-grade, because the mat gives the art breathing room and pulls the eye straight to the mountains. Match the frame finish to the wood and metal tones already in your room rather than to the art itself. If you are starting from scratch, a set of matching thin-profile frames in a single finish keeps a whole wall cohesive and costs far less than buying them one at a time. Standard sizes make the math easy: print at 8x10 and drop it into an 11x14 matted frame, or print at 11x14 for a 16x20 matted frame. Avoid ornate or glossy frames, which fight the calm, minimal feel. Keep the glass clean and hang the print perfectly straight, because crooked art quietly undoes all the effort.
