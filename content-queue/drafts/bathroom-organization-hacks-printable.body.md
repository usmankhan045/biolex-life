## What are the best bathroom organization hacks for a cluttered bathroom?

The best bathroom organization hacks start by going vertical and grouping like with like, because open floor and counter space is the one thing a bathroom never has enough of. Pull everything out, wipe down the surfaces, and sort what you own into small piles, daily essentials, backups, first aid, hair tools, before a single item goes back in. That edit alone usually reveals two or three duplicates and a fistful of expired products you can toss on the spot. Then move up the walls: an over-the-door rack, a tension rod inside the cabinet to hang spray bottles by their triggers, and a slim shelf above the door frame all reclaim storage you didn't know you had. Give each pile its own container so related items travel together and empty out at a glance. The single most effective habit, though, is a one-in-one-out rule for products, a bathroom only stays organized when the sheer volume of stuff stops growing. It also helps to store items where you use them: shower products in the shower, everyday makeup near the mirror, and towels within arm's reach of the tub. Start with these bathroom organization hacks and the counter tends to clear itself within an afternoon.
