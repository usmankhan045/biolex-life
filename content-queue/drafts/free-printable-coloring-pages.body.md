## How do you print coloring pages so they look their best?

Print at actual size on the heaviest paper your printer handles, and the pages come out crisp enough to color with anything. A few settings make the difference: choose "Fit to page" or 100 percent scale so the design is not cropped, print in high quality or "best" mode for sharp lines, and select black-and-white to save your color ink for the coloring itself. Standard copy paper (20 lb) is fine for colored pencils, but if you plan to use markers or gel pens, step up to 28 lb paper or lightweight cardstock so the ink does not bleed through to the next sheet. Print one test page first to check the scale and darkness before you run a whole stack.
