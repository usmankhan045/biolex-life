## What information should a baby shower invitation include?

Every baby shower invitation needs six essentials so guests know exactly what to do: who the shower is for, the date, the start time, the full address, how to RSVP, and the registry details. Missing any one of these creates a flood of follow-up texts you will spend the next week answering. Beyond the basics, add the host's name, whether the shower has a theme or color scheme, and a note if it is a surprise or a couples "sprinkle." Here is a quick checklist to fill before you print:

| Detail | Example |
|---|---|
| Guest of honor | "Celebrating Baby Bennett" |
| Date | Saturday, August 16 |
| Time | 2:00 PM |
| Location | 14 Maple Court, full address |
| RSVP | Text Dana by Aug 9 |
| Registry | Target & Babylist |

Run through the list once, confirm the RSVP deadline gives you a week before the shower, and you will not field a single "wait, where is it again?" the day before.

## How far in advance should you send baby shower invitations?

Send baby shower invitations four to six weeks before the shower date. That window gives guests enough notice to arrange travel, childcare, and gifts without being so early that the date slips their mind. For a shower where many guests travel or the honoree has a large family, lean toward six weeks; for a small, local gathering, four weeks is plenty. Set your RSVP deadline about one to two weeks before the shower so you have a firm headcount for food, seating, and favors. If time is tight because the due date is close or plans came together late, printing at home is your advantage, there is no shipping delay, so you can address and mail invitations the same afternoon you decide on a date. Send a digital heads-up text or save-the-date the moment the date is locked, then follow with the printed invitation as the formal, keepsake version guests can pin to the fridge.

## What is the best paper for printing baby shower invitations at home?

The best paper for a baby shower invitations printable is heavyweight cardstock in the 80 to 110 lb range, which feels substantial in hand and does not go limp in the envelope like copy paper. Plain 20 lb printer paper works in a pinch, but a stack of bright white or soft ivory cardstock is the single upgrade that makes a home-printed invitation read as intentional rather than improvised. Match your envelope size to the card before you print, most fill-in templates are sized to fold to a standard A2 or 4x6, so a box of matching A2 envelopes saves you trimming. Set your printer to its highest quality and "borderless" or "actual size" so the layout does not shrink. Print one test card first to check that the fill-in lines land where you expect, let the ink dry a minute before stacking, and then run the whole batch in one pass.

## How do you word a baby shower invitation?

Word a baby shower invitation with a short, warm headline, then the six key details in a clean stack underneath. The opening line sets the tone, pick one that matches the vibe and let the fill-in template carry the rest. A few reliable options:

- **Classic:** "Please join us for a baby shower honoring Sarah."
- **Sweet:** "A little one is on the way! Celebrate with us."
- **Playful:** "Ready to pop! Come shower Baby Lee with love."
- **Gender-neutral:** "We're welcoming a new little Bennett, come celebrate."
- **Couples' shower:** "Grab a drink and celebrate Mom and Dad-to-be."

After the headline, stack the date, time, address, RSVP line, and registry so the eye moves straight down the card. Keep it to one clear typeface and plenty of white space, a crowded invitation is hard to read at a glance. ## How do you plan the rest of the shower once invitations are out?

Once invitations are mailed, shift to the three things guests will remember: food, games, and favors. Build a simple menu around finger foods and one signature drink so you are not cooking all morning, and set out favors near the door so they are handed off as guests leave. Games are what turn a room of relatives and coworkers into an actual party, baby bingo, a guess-the-baby-food tasting, and a wishes-for-baby card station are low-prep and always land. You can pull a ready-to-print game card from our [free printable activity bingo](/blog/printable-bingo-for-kids) and adapt the squares for a shower in a couple of minutes. Keep a running checklist for the RSVP count, chairs, plates, and a gift-log sheet so someone can note who gave what for thank-you cards later. With the invitations handled early, the last week is decoration and prep, not panic. Print your baby shower invitations printable, put the stamps on, and let the plan carry the rest.
