## How do I print nursery wall art at home so it looks professional?

Print nursery wall art on the heaviest paper your printer allows, at the highest quality setting, with borderless or "fit to frame" turned off so nothing gets cropped. The difference between homemade-looking and framed-shop-quality is almost always the paper. Plain copy paper is thin and shows through; instead reach for 60 to 100 lb matte cardstock or presentation paper, which holds color richly and sits flat behind glass. In your print dialog, choose "Best" or "High" quality, set paper type to matte or cardstock, and select "Actual size" or 100 percent rather than "Scale to fit," which quietly shrinks the margins. If your home printer struggles with color, save the PDF to a thumb drive and print it at a pharmacy or office-supply counter for about a dollar a sheet. Once it is behind glass in a simple frame, no one can tell it started as a free download.
