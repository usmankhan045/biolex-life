## How do you print the Be Still bedroom printable so it looks framed?

Print at 100% scale on the heaviest matte paper your printer allows, and the gap between homemade and store-bought mostly disappears. In your print dialog, choose "Actual size" or set scaling to 100%, never "Fit to page," which quietly shrinks the design and leaves lopsided margins. Set the paper type to "Matte" or "Cardstock" and quality to "Best" so the soft neutral tones come out rich and opaque instead of thin and streaky. The frame does most of the heavy lifting here, so put your effort there: a set of simple, inexpensive gallery frames in one consistent finish (matte black, natural wood, or brushed brass) looks far more expensive than a mix of mismatched frames from the closet. Add a slim white mat inside for an airy, gallery feel that makes even a small print look intentional. Trim any white border cleanly with a paper cutter, and the sharp, even edges read as bought rather than made. If your home printer struggles with larger sizes, most copy shops will print a 16x20 on matte poster stock for a few dollars, which is still a fraction of a framed store print. Either way, the soft neutral tones of this design forgive small printer quirks better than a photo or a bright graphic ever would.

## How do you style Be Still art with the rest of your bedroom?

Keep the palette tight and let the words breathe, because a calm bedroom is about restraint, not quantity. Hang the Be Still print as the quiet centerpiece and build a small, coordinated grouping around it rather than crowding the wall. Pick one or two companion pieces that share its color family, a soft botanical branch or a gentle abstract shape, so the arrangement feels curated instead of collected at random. Lay the frames on the floor first and arrange them before you put a single hole in the wall, leaving two to three inches of breathing room between them and hanging the center at eye level.
