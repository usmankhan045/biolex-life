Everyday Fine-Motor Helpers build strong hands during daily routines
Peel a banana or clementine at snack time
Zip, button & snap their own coat
Squeeze a sponge or washcloth in the bath
Open snack packages, lids & containers
Tear lettuce or snap green beans for dinner
Spray & water plants with a small bottle
Wind up toys, turn keys & twist knobs
Pick up toys with kitchen tongs (make it a game)
Baby & Early Toddler 6-18 months
Pincer-grasp puffs & soft cereal
Drop objects into a bin, then dump & refill
Stack 2-3 soft blocks
Pull scarves from a tissue box
Turn thick board-book pages
Bang two blocks or cups together
Toddler 18 months, 3 years
Stack a tower of 4-6 blocks
Scribble & dot with chunky crayons
Post coins or pom-poms through a slot
Peel & stick large stickers
Roll, poke & squish playdough
Feed self with a spoon or fork
Preschooler 3-4 years
Thread large beads onto a shoelace
Snip paper with safety scissors
Move pom-poms with tongs or tweezers
Build with interlocking bricks
Complete inset & peg puzzles
Trace straight lines, circles & zig-zags
Pre-K & Kindergarten 4-6 years
Cut along curved & zig-zag lines
Hold a pencil with a tripod grip
Lace cards & tie simple knots
Button, zip & snap independently
Draw a person with 6+ body parts
Copy letters, numbers & their name
There's no rush and no "behind." Fine motor skills grow with playful, everyday practice, follow your child's lead and keep it light and fun.
