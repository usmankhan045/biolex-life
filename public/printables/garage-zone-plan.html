Pull everything out of one zone before you sort
Toss what's broken, rusted, dried-up, or expired
Group like with like, all paint, all garden, all sports
Donate duplicate tools and gear you never reach for
Return borrowed items; relocate indoor stuff that drifted in
Drop old paint & chemicals at a hazardous-waste site
Measure walls & ceiling, go vertical to free the floor
