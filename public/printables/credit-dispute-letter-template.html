DEBT VALIDATION LETTER — TO A COLLECTOR

To:  (collection agency name & address)

Re: Account / Reference #

To Whom It May Concern,

I am responding to your contact about a debt you claim I owe. I am requesting validation of this debt under the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act. Please do not contact me again about this matter until you provide the following in writing:

• The amount owed and how it was calculated  • The name of the original creditor  • Proof that you own or are authorized to collect this debt  • A copy of any signed agreement.

Until you provide this validation, please cease all collection activity and do not report this debt to any credit bureau. Please communicate with me only in writing at the address below.

Sincerely,

✓ MAILING CHECKLIST — BEFORE YOU SEND
Send by certified mail with return receipt
This proves the bureau or collector received your letter and when — worth the few dollars.
Keep a copy of every letter you send
Photograph or photocopy the signed letter before it goes in the envelope.
Enclose copies of supporting documents — never originals
Highlight the disputed item on a copy of your credit report if you have one.
Write down the date you mailed it
The 30-day investigation clock starts when the bureau receives your dispute.
Save the return receipt & tracking number
File everything together so you can follow up if you don't hear back.
Set a reminder to follow up in 30–45 days
If the bureau can't verify the item, it must be corrected or removed.
💡 Good to Know
Disputing errors is always free — you never need to pay a "credit repair" company to send these letters for you. Check all three bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion), since an error may appear on only one.
