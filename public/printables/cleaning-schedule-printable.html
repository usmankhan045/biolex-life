🌿 DIY Cleaner Recipes (Cheap & Non-Toxic)
All-purpose: 1 cup water + 1 cup white vinegar + 10 drops lemon oil.
Scrub paste: baking soda + a squeeze of dish soap.
Glass: 2 cups water + 1/2 cup vinegar + 1/4 cup rubbing alcohol.
Fresh drain: 1/2 cup baking soda, then 1 cup hot vinegar.
