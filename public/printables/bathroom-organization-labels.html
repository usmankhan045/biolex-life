Small-Bathroom Zone Plan
Give every item a home, six zones that fit even a tiny bath.
Bathroom
Goal date
Map each zone to a spot, then work the checklist to sort, label, and refill. Page 2 of 2 · Zone Plan
Where each zone lives
Zone 1, Vertical: extra stock, towels, rarely-used items up high
Zone 2, Countertop: only what you touch every day
Zone 3, Under-sink: cleaning supplies, refills, tools
Zone 4, Cabinet: first aid, medicine, small items
Zone 5, Shower: wash, shampoo, shave in a caddy
Zone 6, Linen: folded towels, robes, door hooks
Step 1 · Empty & Sort one drawer at a time
Pull everything out; wipe shelves
Toss empties & expired products
Check dates on meds & sunscreen
Group like with like
Set aside donate / relocate pile
Step 2 · Assign a Zone match to the map
Daily items → countertop (Zone 2)
Backups & towels → up high (Zone 1)
Cleaners & tools → under-sink (Zone 3)
First aid → cabinet (Zone 4)
Add a shower caddy (Zone 5)
Step 3 · Contain & Label use page 1
Add bins, baskets & drawer trays
Cut & attach the printable labels
Install a door or wall hook rack
Decant refills into clear jars
Weekly Reset 2 minutes
Return strays to their zone
Restock paper & refill dispensers
Swap in fresh towels
A small bathroom feels bigger the moment every item has a zone. Start with one drawer today, you don't have to finish it all at once.
