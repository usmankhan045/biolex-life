Linen Closet Zone Map top shelf down, assign a zone to each level
Top
Shelf Rarely used & seasonal, guest bedding, spare blankets, holiday linens. Store in labeled bins so they lift down easily.
Upper
Shelf Bath towels, roll instead of fold to fit more and grab one-handed. Keep your best set at the front.
Mid
Shelf Hand towels & washcloths, corral in an open bin. Add a small basket for extra soap & toilet paper.
Eye-Level
Shelf Sheet sets, tuck each folded set inside its matching pillowcase so complete sets stay together.
Lower
Shelf Toiletry backstock, shampoo, first aid, cleaning refills in labeled bins you can pull out like a drawer.
Floor Bulky & tall items, laundry basket, step stool, vacuum, or an extra pillow. Keep a clear path.
