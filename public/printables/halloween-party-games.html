Party Games to Play check off each one as you go
Mummy Wrap Race
Teams of two race to wrap one player head-to-toe in toilet paper. Fastest tidy mummy wins.
Pumpkin Bowling
Stack empty bottles as pins and roll a small pumpkin to knock them down.
Doughnut on a String
Hang doughnuts from a line and eat them hands-free, no fingers allowed.
Pin the Spider on the Web
Blindfolded players stick a paper spider closest to the center of a drawn web.
Guess the Candy Count
Fill a jar with candy corn. Closest guess takes the whole jar home.
Monster Freeze Dance
Dance while the music plays; freeze like a statue the moment it stops.
Spooky Scavenger Hunt
Hide small toys around the room and send guests off with a picture list.
Eyeball Spoon Race
Balance a ping-pong “eyeball” on a spoon and race to the finish line.
Host tip: set out a small prize table and let winners pick a treat. Keep games short and silly, five to ten minutes each keeps the whole party laughing.
