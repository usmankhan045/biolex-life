How Our Chart Works
1Choose the jobs. Pick 3-6 chores from the lists above and write them on page 1 together.
2Do & check. Each day, color a circle for every job finished, no reminders needed once it's a habit.
3Earn stars. A finished job earns a star. Color one in the Star Bank and watch them add up.
4Celebrate. Hit the star goal and unlock the reward, then start a fresh chart next week.
